Local Presence

Construction Services for Tambaram Homeowners

Tambaram is one of South Chennai's most established residential hubs — a busy railway junction with easy access to GST Road (NH45), Chennai city, and the airport. That connectivity has made it home to generations of independent houses, many of which are now due for reconstruction or renovation, alongside newer plotted development on the town's outer edges.

BDX Smart Homes builds and renovates homes for Tambaram families from our South Chennai base in nearby Guduvanchery, bringing the same end-to-end approach — architecture, structural design, and construction under one roof — to both fresh plot construction and the rebuilding of older Tambaram homes.
