About This Project

Project Overview

SK Residence is a contemporary 5 BHK G+2 residence in Kayarambedu, designed to balance bold architectural character with comfortable modern living. Its distinctive façade combines exposed red brick, textured grey frames, clean white geometric volumes, and terracotta jaali screens, creating a warm and timeless identity. Spacious balconies, open terraces, greenery, and deep projections improve shade, privacy, natural light, and ventilation.
