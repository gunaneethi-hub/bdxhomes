Home Construction Along Vandalur's Growing GST Road Corridor

Vandalur sits on the GST Road (NH45) corridor between Tambaram and Urapakkam, giving it strong connectivity to Chennai city, the airport, and the industrial belt further south. Over the past few years the area has seen a steady rise in plotted residential layouts, drawing families who want an independent home within easy reach of established South Chennai neighborhoods.

BDX Smart Homes is headquartered a short drive away in Guduvanchery, with an active project base in nearby Urapakkam — that proximity means our design and site teams can visit Vandalur plots frequently, supervise construction closely, and respond quickly whenever homeowners need us.
