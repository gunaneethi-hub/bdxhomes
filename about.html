Building Dream Homes in South Chennai Since 2014

We're a residential construction company built on one belief — your home should be designed and built exactly the way you imagined it, with no compromises and no hidden costs.

Our Story

From a Single Plot to 100+ Homes Delivered

BDX Smart Homes began in 2014 with a simple goal: help families in South Chennai build homes on their own plots without the stress, delays, and hidden costs that plague most construction projects. What started as a small team of architects and engineers has grown into a full-service residential construction company trusted by over 100 families across Guduvanchery, Tambaram, Chrompet, and the wider South Chennai region.

Today, we handle everything from architectural design and CMDA/DTCP approvals through to construction, interiors, and after-sales support — all under one roof, with one point of contact, and one promise: the home you're shown is the home you get.
