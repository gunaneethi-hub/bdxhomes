Our Story

From Architecture to Smarter Home Construction

Our journey began in 2013 with A4H Architects, starting with architectural design and planning. As our experience grew, we expanded into construction and project execution, learning first-hand the challenges of turning architectural designs into real homes.

We realised that a great design is only the beginning. Bridging the gap between what is designed, what a homeowner understands and what is finally built requires better planning, coordination and communication.

Today, BDX brings together Architecture, 5D BIM, 3D Visualisation, VR, Construction and Project Management to help homeowners plan, visualise and build their homes with greater clarity.
