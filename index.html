Services

Our Services

From architectural planning to final handover, BDX Smart Homes delivers end-to-end residential construction services across South Chennai — on time, on budget, and built to last.

Architecture Design

Vaastu-compliant architectural designs with 3D visualisation, structural drawings, and CMDA / DTCP approval assistance — tailored for homes in Chennai and South Chennai. Our architects translate your vision into precise floor plans and elevations, balancing aesthetics with local bye-law compliance so approvals are smooth and construction begins on time.

Residential Construction

Plot-to-home construction across Guduvanchery, Tambaram, Chrompet, and South Chennai. RCC structure, Grade 1 materials, 150+ quality checks, and a 10-year structural warranty. Dedicated site engineers, transparent billing, and real-time progress updates — your home handed over on schedule, fully complete and ready to move in.

Home Interiors

Complete home interiors in Chennai — modular kitchens, false ceilings, wardrobes, pooja rooms, and living spaces — designed to match your budget and lifestyle. 3D renders let you see every detail before work begins, and factory-finished materials ensure a clean, stress-free handover.

Project Management

Dedicated project managers, real-time site updates via mobile app, daily photo reports, and strict milestone tracking — so your Chennai home is delivered on schedule. We coordinate contractors, materials, and inspections so you stay in full control without visiting the site every day.
