Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

1. Who We Are

BDX Smart Homes Pvt Ltd ("BDX", "we", "us", or "our") is a residential construction company based in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India. Our registered office is at 717, Nellikupam Road, Near SIS Queenstown, Kayarambedu, Guduvanchery, Chennai — 603202. You can reach us at admin@bdxhomes.com or +91 91500 07269.

2. What Information We Collect

We collect personal information only when you voluntarily provide it through our website forms:

  • Contact & consultation forms — name, phone number, email address, and service interest
  • Job application forms — name, phone number, email address, role applied for, and uploaded resume

We do not collect sensitive personal data such as financial information, government ID numbers, or health data.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to:

  • Respond to your consultation or service enquiry
  • Contact you about your home construction project
  • Process and follow up on job applications
  • Send relevant updates about BDX Smart Homes services (only if you have contacted us)

We do not use your information for automated decision-making or profiling.

4. Advertising & Tracking

We may run advertising campaigns on Meta (Facebook & Instagram) and Google. These platforms may use cookies and tracking pixels to show our ads to relevant audiences. This includes:

  • Meta Pixel — tracks visits to our website to measure ad performance and show relevant ads on Facebook and Instagram
  • Google Ads — tracks conversions and may show our ads across Google Search and partner sites

You can opt out of interest-based advertising through your Facebook Ad Settings or Google Ad Settings.

5. Cookies

Our website may use cookies for basic functionality and analytics. Cookies are small text files stored on your device. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time, though this may affect how some parts of the site work.

6. How We Store & Protect Your Data

Your information is stored securely and accessed only by authorised BDX team members who need it to respond to your enquiry. We do not store payment information on our website. We retain your contact details for as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose for which they were provided, after which they are deleted.

7. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only with:

  • Our internal team members handling your enquiry or application
  • Technology service providers (e.g. email, CRM) who process data on our behalf under strict confidentiality obligations
  • Legal or regulatory authorities if required by law

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Withdraw consent to be contacted at any time

To exercise any of these rights, email us at admin@bdxhomes.com.

9. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external websites (e.g. Google Maps, social media). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to read their privacy policies.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page with the date it was last updated. Continued use of our website after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
